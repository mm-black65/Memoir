Team sync — product roadmap discussion Q3 2025

18 min 42 sec 1,847 words ~8 min read ✓ AI transcribed

Good morning, everyone. Thank you all for joining today's product roadmap sync. We have a packed agenda and I want to make sure we cover all the key initiatives before end of quarter.

The engineering team has made significant progress on the core recording pipeline. We've reduced upload latency by approximately forty percent compared to last month, which is a major win for users in low-connectivity areas. The background sync worker is now much more resilient to intermittent network conditions.

On the AI transcription front, we've been evaluating three different providers — Google Speech-to-Text, Deepgram, and OpenAI Whisper. Our benchmarks show Deepgram leading on accuracy for conversational speech, but Google has the edge on longer monologues and lectures. We'll likely offer both as user-selectable options.

The mobile experience has been a point of contention in user feedback. Several power users have requested a dedicated reading mode for transcripts — something that feels closer to a Kindle than a dashboard. We're planning a typography overhaul and a distraction-free reader view for the next release.

Regarding the pen hardware itself — the team in Shenzhen has finalized the PCB layout for revision two. Battery life has improved to roughly fourteen hours of standby and approximately three hours of continuous recording. The tactile button feel has also been refined based on prototype feedback from our early access group.

Storage management remains a challenge. We need a smarter auto-archiving policy that deletes processed audio from the device once transcripts are confirmed saved locally. Currently users have to manage this manually which creates unnecessary friction.

The design team will share mockups for the notification timeline and the device settings panel in the next sprint review. We're aiming for something that feels as polished as Apple Settings — clean, purposeful, no engineering jargon.
